# Catapult (Teardown mod)

A giant wooden trebuchet that throws boulders, barrels, cars, crates and you. Russian: [README.ru.md](README.ru.md).

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| LMB | place a catapult 14 m in front of you, facing where you look (up to 4) |
| Q, or E on the machine | fire (E works with any tool in hand; F is the game's flashlight) |
| Mouse wheel | power (tool switching is locked while a catapult is selected) |
| Z / X | turn the machine left / right (C is the game's camera view) |
| V / B | lower / raise the angle (20-70°, default 45°) |
| 1 2 3 4 | load a boulder / explosive barrel / car / pile of crates |
| RMB | get into the bucket yourself: the catapult fires at once and throws you along the arc (the bucket's own load is taken out first) |
| R | remove all catapults and the ammo they spawned |

The catapult you are nearest to (within 40 m) is selected: it gets an outline, the HUD shows power, angle, ammo and state, and a dashed **arc preview** with a ring shows where the load will land (and the range in metres).

## How it is built

- One prefab spawned in front of you: a **static wooden frame** (about 13 m tall with the counterweight up, real destructible voxels) with an iron axle, and the **arm** - a dynamic body made of a cube hub, a two-piece beam, a bucket and a hollow counterweight.
- **Assembly by measurement.** Where the game puts a spawned vox relative to its `pos` depends on the model's size (in-game 16.10: along the model's MagicaVoxel y the pivot lies floor(n/2)+1 voxels from the low side, so a 4-voxel probe reads 0.75, the 30-voxel beam 0.53), so no single pivot fraction fits every part - the beam came out 1.5 m low and 1.1 m back. Now the whole machine is first spawned still, 60 m up, the real centre of every part is read (`GetShapeBounds`), the difference to its designed centre is added to that part's `pos`, and it is spawned once more to check. Then the real machine is spawned on the ground and checked the same way (spawned again if any part is more than 5 cm off). The corrections are kept for the next catapults. Parts are recognised by their sizes in any axis order; if anything is missing after 30 ticks, everything spawned is deleted again.
- **The arm turns by script** about the measured axle centre, not on a spawned hinge joint: in-game the joint from the spawn text held no shape at all (`joint shapes 0`, `hub-nothing`), and what such a joint attaches to cannot be set by script. Every physics tick the arm body is put at its pose for the current arm angle, with the matching angular velocity. The arm's shapes do not collide with the frame's (`SetShapeCollisionFilter`), so the hub around the axle cannot jam.
- **Firing**: the arm swings up at about 290°/s. Everything in the bucket - bodies and players - is carried with the bucket while it swings (moved with it every physics tick, bodies and players alike) and released when the arm reaches the set angle (90° minus the angle above the horizontal, behind the axle). At the release the mod's own load is thrown as a fresh copy of itself (a load that lay still in the bucket is out of the simulation, and in-game nothing brought it back: it hung in the air at the release point), and every load gets exactly the planned speed, 14 m/s at power 0 to 56 m/s at full power, so range and the arc preview agree. Half a second later the arm goes back to rest and the catapult reloads (option).
- **Ammo**: boulder (1.8 m of rock), explosive barrel (explodes where it lands, but never within 12 m of the catapult), a car - the game's own small car prefab if it can be spawned, otherwise the mod's car body - and a pile of six crates. Ammo is placed by measurement too: once it exists, its real bounds are read and it is moved so that it lies on the bucket floor (the car, wider than the bucket, on the rim), then held there gently while the catapult stands. Anything else put into the bucket, including you, is thrown too; a launched player gets no fall damage by default.
- **Impacts**: losing more than 45 % of the speed in one step while flying faster than 12 m/s is a hit. Holes scale with speed and the load (boulder 1, car 0.85, barrel 0.7, crates 0.5): soft materials always, brick and concrete from 25 m/s, hard materials from 45 m/s; a boulder punches two more holes along its path. Up to 3 hits per load.
- **Breaking it and removing it**: the frame and the arm are ordinary voxels; explosions break them. If the hub, the axle or a body of the machine is destroyed, the catapult is marked broken and does not fire. Every body and shape the mod spawns is tagged (`catapult_part`, `catapult_ammo`), and R deletes everything with those tags - also pieces broken off, catapults the mod lost track of, and parts left by the first version (tags `catapult`, `catapult_arm`).

Limits: at most 4 catapults, 24 mod-spawned ammo bodies (the oldest go first), 3 cars, 24 tracked flying loads, 8 holes per physics step; the arc preview is recomputed 6-7 times a second (up to about 80 raycasts in that frame).

## Options

| Setting | Default | Key |
|---|---|---|
| Ammo at start: boulder / barrel / car / crates | boulder | `savegame.mod.ammo` |
| Reload automatically | on | `savegame.mod.reload` |
| Impact damage x0.25-x2 | x1 | `savegame.mod.damage` |
| Fall damage for a launched player | off | `savegame.mod.falldamage` |
| Arc preview | on | `savegame.mod.arc` |
| Debug line (for testing) | off | `savegame.mod.debugline` |

The debug line (option "Debug line"; bottom left, while the tool is in hand and a catapult is selected) has three lines: `catapult: <state> | parts off m: frame .. axle .. hub .. beam_long .. beam_short .. bucket .. weight .. | spawns N` - how far each part is from where it should be (all about 0.00 when the machine is built right; N counts the measuring spawns); `hinge script hub-axle 0.00 m (no joint) | arm angle -54 (rest -54) | last shot: angle -54 -> +45, released 6 at 35 m/s | fire: Q or E` - the hub's distance from the axle's axis (`hub OFF the axle` if over 5 cm), the arm angle (grows while firing) and the last shot; `load 6/6 in bucket | crates: Spawn gave 6 entities, 6 bodies | placed 6/6, moved into place by 0.31 m, lowest 0.02 m above the floor`. While a catapult is being placed, and after a refused placement, a fourth line shows the stage or the reason: `placing: measure (spawn n)`, `placing: assemble, waiting: missing ... (tick n)`, `refused: missing ... among N bodies / M shapes after 30 ticks`, `refused: no ground ...`.

Second in-game check of 17.10, after 80eed03 ("the catapult sends me, but I fly only half the way, then the speed runs out, and not the same every time; the loads now fly about 5 m further and stop"), debug line `released 6 at 46 m/s | respawned awake 32, stuck 24`: the fresh copies hung too, and the stuck fix moved them on its arc only 3 times (about 5 m). The game wakes a body with an impulse (`data/script/awake.lua`: a zero `ApplyBodyImpulse`) and sets a thrown body's velocity in tick (snowball, skeet), the mod did it once in update. Now every launch velocity goes with a waking impulse and is set once more in the first tick; a load out of the simulation (`IsBodyActive` false - asleep, hanging) is carried along its arc for the whole flight, not 3 times, until the arc runs into something - that is its hit (holes, explosion) and it drops there; a load that stopped in the simulation is a real hit, found by its loss of speed as before. The launched player is given the arc's velocity every tick until he lands or the arc is blocked. Stand: `H.sleepModel.impulseWakes = false` (nothing wakes a sleeping load) and `H.playerAirDrag = 1.5` (the player's air movement eats a set velocity); `tests/flight_law.lua` and `tests/ride_law.lua` are red without the carrying and without the per-tick player arc (the player lands 38 and 185 m short).

In-game check of 17.10 after 2a46c03 ("the car finally fires properly, the other loads just hang in the air; RMB load should load me into the catapult and send me"): the boulder, barrel and crates, asleep in the bucket, were moved to the release point and nothing - not even `SetBodyActive(true)` - put them back into the simulation; the car, a vehicle, flew. Now the load is carried with the bucket by `SetBodyTransform`, thrown as a fresh spawned copy (awake, like the game's own snowball and skeet), and a thrown load that does not move is put on its arc again (debug line: `respawned awake N, stuck M, rides K`). RMB now rides: the player is put on the bucket floor (its own load removed), the catapult fires at once, the player passes through the catapult (`CollisionMask`) for 1 s and is thrown along the arc. Stand: `H.sleepModel.activeWakes = false, vehicleAwake = true` reproduces the screenshot (loads at 0 m/s, the car flies); `tests/flight_law.lua` (now also: carried with the bucket, no old copy left, a load left asleep after the throw still lands on the arc) and the new `tests/ride_law.lua` are red on 2a46c03 (12 and 10 checks); mutants 8/9 (the one not caught: the player not moved to the release point at the release tick - he is there within one step anyway).

Fourth in-game report (16.10: "the ammo spawns crooked and nothing works; F is the flashlight"), debug line `pivot 0.50 0.50 0.74 | hinge hub-nothing | joint shapes 0 | parts off hub -0.0/0.0/0.1 beam_long -0.0/-1.5/1.1 beam_short -0.0/-0.2/0.2`: the pivot of a spawned vox depends on the model's size, so the probe's fraction misplaced the parts, and the spawned hinge held nothing. Fixed by measuring every part and correcting it (see How it is built), turning the arm by script, placing the ammo by its measured bounds and moving fire to Q (F is the flashlight, C the camera view - data/input_settings.xml). The harness spawns voxes like the game did (`pivot = "field"`, `jointsAttach = false`); `tests/field_parts_law.lua` first checks the stand against the screenshot's numbers, then: every part within 5 cm, the hub on the axle and still there 1 s later, the debug line saying so, six crates inside the bucket and not sinking, F does not fire, Q fires, the arm angle grows, all six crates thrown, C does not turn the machine. Red on the old code (25 checks); mutants without the correction, without the scripted arm, without the ammo placement and with fire back on F are all red.

Second in-game report ("no surface is accepted"): the placement checked everything in the spawn tick and gave up at once. Now it is spread over ticks: the pivot probe is read for up to 20 ticks (then the centre-pivot fallback), the parts are found among the handles Spawn returned (shape -> body with `GetShapeBody`, not `GetBodyShapes`) for up to 30 ticks, and only then is anything refused - with the reason on screen. `tests/strict_law.lua` models a stricter game (zero bounds, no body shapes and no dynamic flag in the spawn tick, filled-voxel bounds, corner pivot).

Third in-game report (10.10: "it spawns only partly, complains about bad ground and disappears almost at once", `refused: arm, bucket not found among 1 bodies / 2 shapes after 30 ticks`): the catapult was spawned from an XML text with two top-level `<body>` elements side by side (the static frame with frame and axle, then the dynamic arm with hub, hinge, beam, bucket and weight). An XML text has one root element, and the game spawned only the first: the frame alone, 1 body and 2 shapes. The tags and the hinge were fine; the arm was simply never created. Now every multi-body spawn (the catapult, the pivot probe, the six crates) is one document wrapped the way the game's own scripts do it, `<prefab version='1.2.0'><group ...>...</group></prefab>` (`data/script/mp/lib/tools.lua`). A failed assembly no longer says "aim at open ground": it says the catapult did not assemble and was removed (EN/RU, 6 s); "no room here: aim at open ground" stays for a missing ground. The technical reason goes to the game's log (`print`, lines starting with `catapult:`). The debug line is off by default now, under a new key `debugline` (an old saved `debug = true` does not bring it back). `tests/field_assembly_law.lua` replays the report (red on the old code with exactly that line): one prefab document, a static frame and a separate dynamic arm with its tag, the 9x9x5 bucket on the arm, the hinge on the arm and holding, six crates in one load, the no-ground and did-not-assemble messages are different, the reason is logged, nothing of a failed catapult is left, no debug line by default. The harness now spawns only the first top-level element of a text with several and reports it as an error, so every mod's tests catch this.

## Install, build, test

```powershell
New-Item -ItemType Junction -Path "$env:USERPROFILE\Documents\Teardown\mods\catapult" -Target "$PWD\mod"   # run inside the mod folder
..\_shared\.venv\Scripts\python tools\build_all.py
```

The harness models the game's spawn (vox pivot at the bottom-face centre, transform reported one tick late) and the hinge motor. `tests/throw_law.lua`: range grows with power - 63-64, 107-109, 166-169, 239-242 and 328-330 m at power 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8 and 1.0 (at least 150 m required at full power); the load leaves at the planned speed (within 2 %), is clear of the bucket 10 frames later and nothing holds it; the arc preview lands within 5 % of the real landing; a player in the bucket flies 329 m without fall damage. `tests/assembly_law.lua` and `tests/assembly_corner_law.lua` reproduce the in-game screenshot (vox centred on its pos; and the corner variant): the frame must stand on the ground, the axle sit on it at 9.6 m, the hinge be attached and the arm hang still with the bucket above the ground - the old code fails exactly like the screenshot (frame bottom at -4.8 m, axle at 9.0 m above a 4.8 m frame, hinge not attached). `tests/removal_law.lua`: with body tags unreadable, a planted broken piece and a part of the first version, R must leave nothing (the old code left 2 bodies and could not register the catapult). `tests/impact_law.lua`: boulders hitting a wood and a brick wall at 37 m/s break holes of 18-19 m² and 10-11 m²; crates, barrel and car are thrown, the barrel explodes; E, turning and the angle work; a broken hinge stops the machine; at most 4 catapults and 24 mod ammo bodies even after six crate volleys; R removes everything. Every check fails when the matching code is broken (mutation check).

**Needs an in-game check**: that the measured assembly converges in the real game (the debug line's `parts off` all about 0.00 and `spawns` 3), that the scripted arm swings smoothly and the load stays in the bucket until the release, and whether the game's car prefab path works.

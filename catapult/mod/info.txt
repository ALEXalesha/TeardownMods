description = A giant wooden trebuchet, about 13 m tall, that you place in front of you. Its frame is real destructible wood and its arm swings on a real hinge. Whatever lies in the bucket gets thrown: a boulder, an explosive barrel, a car, a pile of crates - or you. Controls: LMB - place a catapult in front of you; F, or E on the machine - fire; Mouse wheel - power; Z / C - turn left / right; X / V - lower / raise the angle; 1 2 3 4 - load a boulder / barrel / car / crates; RMB - load the selected ammo; R - remove all catapults.

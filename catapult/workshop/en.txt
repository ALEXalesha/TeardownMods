[h1]Catapult[/h1]
A giant wooden trebuchet, about 13 m tall, that you place in front of you. Its frame is real destructible wood and its arm swings about the axle. Whatever lies in the bucket gets thrown: a boulder, an explosive barrel, a car, a pile of crates - or you.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Full-power boulder flies about 300 m (power on the mouse wheel, angle 20-70°)
[*]Predicted arc and landing ring before you fire
[*]Impacts punch holes by speed and material: wood easily, brick from 25 m/s, hard materials from 45 m/s; boulders punch through, barrels explode
[*]Ride it: RMB puts you into the bucket and launches you along the arc (no fall damage by default)
[*]The machine can be blown apart; up to 4 catapults at once
[*]Own models and sounds: creaking wood, the swing, the release, whistling flight, impacts
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB[/b] - place a catapult in front of you
[*][b]Q, or E on the machine[/b] - fire
[*][b]Mouse wheel[/b] - power
[*][b]Z / X[/b] - turn left / right
[*][b]V / B[/b] - lower / raise the angle
[*][b]1 2 3 4[/b] - load a boulder / barrel / car / crates
[*][b]RMB[/b] - get into the bucket and be launched
[*][b]R[/b] - remove all catapults
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Ammo at start: [i]Boulder[/i]
[*]Reload automatically: [i]on[/i]
[*]Impact damage x0.25-x2: [i]x1[/i]
[*]Fall damage for a launched player: [i]off[/i]
[*]Arc preview: [i]on[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]The throw speed is set by the mod at the release (so power and range are exact); the arm's swing itself is turned by the script about the axle
[*]A car is the game's own small car if its prefab can be spawned, otherwise the mod's car body
[*]At most 24 mod-spawned ammo pieces exist at once; the oldest disappear
[/list]

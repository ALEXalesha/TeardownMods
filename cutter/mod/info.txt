en_description = An angle-grinder tool with a spinning blade. Hold LMB on a surface, sweep to the end point and release: the saw cuts a clean, narrow straight kerf through walls, doors and vehicles, hole by hole, until it comes out the other side or reaches the max depth. By default it cuts wood, glass, plastic, plaster and metal; concrete and brick are cut too, but slower; hard metal is off. Sparks, sawdust, a motor whine and a grinding loop whose pitch follows the material. Options: kerf width, depth, cut length, reach, speed (holes per step), material switches, sparks.

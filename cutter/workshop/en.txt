[h1]Cutter[/h1]
An angle grinder. Hold the mouse button on a surface, sweep to the end point and release: the saw cuts a straight kerf from one point to the other, through walls, floors and vehicles.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Dashed preview line while you hold
[*]Cuts through to the other side, up to the max depth
[*]Wood, glass, plaster, plastic; metal and concrete too (concrete slower); hard metal optional
[*]Sparks on metal and concrete, sawdust on wood, dust on concrete
[*]Motor whine, spin-up and spin-down, grinding pitch that depends on the material
[*]Up to 4 queued cuts
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hold LMB, sweep, release[/b] - cut a straight line from the press point to the release point
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Kerf width 0.1-0.4 m: [i]0.2 m[/i]
[*]Max depth 0.2-3 m: [i]1 m[/i]
[*]Max cut length 0.5-10 m: [i]4 m[/i]
[*]Reach 2-15 m: [i]6 m[/i]
[*]Speed (holes per step) 4-40: [i]12[/i]
[*]Cuts metal: [i]yes[/i]
[*]Cuts concrete and brick: [i]yes[/i]
[*]Concrete is slower by x1-x6: [i]x3[/i]
[*]Cuts hard metal: [i]no[/i]
[*]Sparks and dust: [i]yes[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Hard metal and hard masonry are off by default
[*]Cutting speed is limited by a per-step budget
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).

en_description = A deployable quadcopter with spinning rotors, a hum that follows the thrust and a light. LMB launches it and switches you to its camera while your body stays behind: WASD to fly, Space/Ctrl up and down, mouse to look, LMB drops a bomb on the target marker below (the Singularity Grenade if that mod is enabled, otherwise a built-in bomb), Q returns the view, P photo mode (hides the HUD; take the picture with Steam's screenshot key), R packs the drone up when you stand next to it. Battery and signal range are limited: near the edge the picture gets static, past it the drone loses the link and descends. Flying into something too fast breaks it.

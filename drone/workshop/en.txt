[h1]Camera Drone[/h1]
A deployable quadcopter flown from its own camera while your body stays behind. Battery, signal range with static, a target marker and bombs - or Singularity Grenades if that mod is on.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Holds its position and altitude with no input; steady at any frame rate
[*]Battery: when it runs out the motors stop and the drone falls
[*]Signal range: static near the edge, lost link beyond it
[*]Hitting something too fast breaks it into debris
[*]Target marker: where a bomb released now would land
[*]Photo mode hides the HUD
[*]Own voxel model, rotor blur, blinking LEDs, hum
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB (on foot)[/b] - launch the drone / take control again
[*][b]R (on foot, within 4 m)[/b] - pack it up (refills bombs and battery)
[*][b]WASD[/b] - fly
[*][b]Space / Ctrl[/b] - up / down
[*][b]Mouse[/b] - look and turn
[*][b]LMB (flying)[/b] - drop a bomb
[*][b]Q[/b] - back to your body (the drone keeps hovering)
[*][b]P[/b] - photo mode
[/list]

[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Photo mode only hides the HUD: take the picture with Steam's screenshot key (F12)
[*]Your body stays where you left it and can be hurt
[*]If the game does not move the drone physically, it switches to a fallback flight mode; in that mode it stops at walls instead of bouncing off them
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).

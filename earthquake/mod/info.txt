The ground shakes loose objects and vehicles, the camera shakes, dust rises and grit falls from ceilings; stronger quakes crack glass, wood and plaster, then concrete and brick, and loosen hinges and ropes, so weak buildings fail on their own. Optional aftershocks. Options: duration, starting preset, radius, aftershocks, structural damage, camera shake, epicentre.

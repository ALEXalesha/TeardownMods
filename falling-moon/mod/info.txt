en_description = A telescope tool. LMB calls the moon down on the aim point: over 1-5 minutes it grows in the sky, the ground starts to tremble, wind picks up, light debris lifts off, the sky turns red and a countdown runs. At the end: a blinding flash, a huge crater with a glowing lunar boulder, and a shockwave that rolls outwards and flattens everything in a large radius, starting fires, followed by drifting dust while the sky slowly recovers. RMB during the approach sends the moon back. Options: time until impact, shockwave radius, damage, fires, red sky, tremors and wind, countdown.

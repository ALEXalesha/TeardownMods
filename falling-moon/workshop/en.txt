[h1]Falling Moon[/h1]
A telescope that calls the moon down on the aim point. Over 1-5 minutes it grows in the sky, the ground trembles, the sky turns red and a countdown runs; for the last seconds it is a real voxel moon that comes down and takes almost everything around with it.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]A real 3D moon: shaded and hidden behind trees while far away, a real voxel sphere for the last seconds
[*]Tremors, wind and lifting debris build up; red sky; countdown with beeps and a roar
[*]Impact: blinding flash, a crater around the embedded moon
[*]Shockwave that takes down almost everything in the radius, hard materials near the centre too
[*]Explosions, fires, objects thrown hard, dust; the sky recovers slowly
[*]Send it back with RMB while it is still far away
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB[/b] - call the moon down on the aim point
[*][b]RMB[/b] - send it back (while it is still far away)
[/list]

[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Very heavy on big maps: if the game stutters, pick a lower destruction level or a smaller moon
[*]The moon stays in its crater afterwards
[/list]

[h1]Falling Moon[/h1]
A telescope that calls the moon down on the aim point. Over 1-5 minutes it grows in the sky, the ground trembles, dust and wind rise and a countdown runs; then it slams into the ground, sits in a huge crater as a real voxel moon, and its shockwave takes almost everything around with it.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]A solid-looking moon that hides behind trees and buildings, with an orange heat glow on its edge
[*]No flickering: the sky is left alone; tremors, wind, lifted dust and a growing camera shake build up instead
[*]Countdown with beeps and a roar
[*]Impact: blinding flash, a real voxel moon in a huge crater, a shockwave ring
[*]Shockwave that takes down practically everything within 200 m, hard materials near the centre too
[*]Explosions, fires, objects thrown hard, dust; send it back with RMB before it lands
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB[/b] - call the moon down on the aim point
[*][b]RMB[/b] - send it back
[/list]

[list]

[/list]

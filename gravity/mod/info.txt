en_description = Press G (or K, see Options) to switch the gravity of the whole level: Earth, Moon, Mars, zero-G, reverse (things fall up) and sideways (towards your right). Hold it to go back to Earth. The change is smooth, uses the game's real gravity setting, and wakes resting objects around you so they react. In zero-G you float and Space/Ctrl move you up and down. An indicator shows the preset, the strength and an arrow pointing to where "down" is. Options: key, transition time, reverse and sideways strength, automatic return to Earth, wake radius, thrusters, indicator.

[h1]Gravity Change[/h1]
Switch the real gravity of the whole level with one key: Earth, Moon, Mars, zero-G, reverse and sideways, with a smooth transition. Works with anything in hand.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Six presets: Earth, Moon (1.62 m/s²), Mars (3.71), zero-G, reverse, sideways
[*]Smooth transition; resting objects nearby are woken so they react
[*]Zero-G thrusters: float up and down
[*]Indicator with an arrow pointing to where down is
[*]Floating dust, a soft hum in zero-G, a sweep sound on each change
[*]Optional automatic return to Earth
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]G (tap)[/b] - next preset
[*][b]G (hold 0.8 s)[/b] - back to Earth
[*][b]Space / Ctrl in zero-G[/b] - float up / down
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Key G / J / K: [i]G[/i]
[*]Transition 0-5 s: [i]1.5 s[/i]
[*]Reverse strength: [i]4 m/s²[/i]
[*]Sideways strength: [i]6 m/s²[/i]
[*]Back to Earth after never / 10 / 30 / 60 s: [i]never[/i]
[*]Wake objects within: [i]60 m[/i]
[*]Zero-G thrusters: [i]on[/i]
[*]Indicator: [i]show[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Gravity goes back to normal when the level restarts
[*]Objects farther than the wake radius keep sleeping until something touches them
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).

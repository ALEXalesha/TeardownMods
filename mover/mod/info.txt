Hold LMB on any loose object, piece of debris or vehicle to grab it, mouse wheel pushes and pulls it along your view, Q/R, Z/X, V/B rotate it on three axes (smooth or snapped), T freezes it in place or unfreezes it, P clones it next to the original, N/M resamples its voxels to half or double size (experimental, small objects), U undoes the last move.

en_description = A gizmo gun for moving things. Hold LMB on any loose object, piece of debris or vehicle to grab it, mouse wheel pushes and pulls it along your view, Q/R, Z/X, V/B rotate it on three axes (smooth or snapped), T freezes it in place or unfreezes it, G clones it next to the original, N/M resamples its voxels to half or double size (experimental, small objects), U undoes the last move. The held object gets an outline and the controls are shown on screen. Options: distance, speed, position and rotation snapping, rotation speed, clone and scale switches, help panel.
ru_description = Пушка-гизмо для перемещения предметов. Схваченный предмет подсвечивается контуром, подсказка клавиш на экране. Настройки: дальность, скорость, привязка к сетке и шаг поворота, скорость поворота, клонирование и масштаб, панель подсказок.

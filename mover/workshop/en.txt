[h1]Mover[/h1]
A gizmo gun that moves things: grab any loose object or vehicle, push and pull it, rotate it on three axes, freeze it in mid-air, clone it, resample it and undo your moves.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Carried objects still collide with the world
[*]Vehicles are moved as a whole
[*]Optional position and rotation snapping
[*]Undo for the last 12 moves, freezes and clones
[*]Outlines for aimed, held and frozen objects; a controls panel
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hold LMB[/b] - grab the aimed object; release drops it
[*][b]Mouse wheel[/b] - push / pull
[*][b]Q / R[/b] - rotate
[*][b]Z / X[/b] - tilt
[*][b]V / B[/b] - roll
[*][b]T[/b] - freeze / unfreeze
[*][b]G[/b] - clone
[*][b]N / M[/b] - resample x0.5 / x2 (experimental)
[*][b]U[/b] - undo
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Max distance 5-100 m: [i]40 m[/i]
[*]Move speed: [i]20[/i]
[*]Position snap: [i]off[/i]
[*]Rotation snap: [i]off[/i]
[*]Rotation speed: [i]90 °/s[/i]
[*]Cloning: [i]on[/i]
[*]Scaling: [i]on[/i]
[*]Controls panel: [i]show[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Static level geometry (buildings, terrain) cannot be grabbed
[*]Clones do not copy joints, lights or scripts; vehicles cannot be cloned
[*]Scaling resamples voxels (small objects, up to 128 voxels per side) and cannot be undone
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).

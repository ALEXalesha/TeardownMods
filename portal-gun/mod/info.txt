en_description = A portal gun. LMB places the blue portal, RMB the orange one, on flat surfaces: walls, floors and ceilings. Walk, fall or throw objects into one and they come out of the other with their momentum turned to match the exit. Glowing animated rims, rim sparks, sounds for firing, opening, closing and passing through, a hand-held gun. R closes both portals. A portal closes by itself when the surface under it is destroyed. Teardown cannot render a view through a portal, so the portal surface is an animated swirl. Options: range, portal size, objects pass through, heaviest object, portals on moving objects, hum volume.

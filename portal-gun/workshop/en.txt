[h1]Portal Gun[/h1]
Place a blue and an orange portal on flat surfaces and walk, fall or throw things through them. Momentum and direction are kept, so floor-to-wall flings work.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Player and objects pass through with their speed
[*]Floor, wall and ceiling portals
[*]No ping-pong: a short cooldown and you must step away first
[*]A portal closes when its surface is destroyed
[*]Animated swirl, glowing rim, sparks, hum and sounds
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB[/b] - blue portal
[*][b]RMB[/b] - orange portal
[*][b]R[/b] - close both
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Range 20-200 m: [i]100 m[/i]
[*]Portal size small / normal / large: [i]normal[/i]
[*]Objects pass through: [i]yes[/i]
[*]Heaviest object 50-3000 kg: [i]500 kg[/i]
[*]Portals on moving objects: [i]no[/i]
[*]Portal hum volume: [i]0.5[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]You cannot see through a portal: Teardown has no render-to-texture
[*]Vehicles are not teleported
[*]Objects bigger than the portal simply hit the wall
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).

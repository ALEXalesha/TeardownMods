en_description = An RC transmitter for any vehicle. Aim at a car and press LMB to drive it remotely while you stay where you are: W/S throttle and reverse, A/D steer, Space handbrake, V toggles the chase camera (mouse orbits it), Q lets go, LMB on another vehicle hops to it.

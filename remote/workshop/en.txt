[h1]Vehicle Remote[/h1]
Point at any vehicle and drive it remotely, like an RC car, while you stay where you are. A chase camera, a signal range with static, and hopping from car to car.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Real driving through the game's vehicle controls
[*]Chase camera behind the car, mouse orbits it, kept out of walls
[*]Signal range: static near the edge; past it the car brakes and is released
[*]Outlines for the controlled and aimed car; HUD with speed, damage and signal
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB on a vehicle[/b] - take control; on another one while driving: hop to it
[*][b]W / S[/b] - throttle / reverse
[*][b]A / D[/b] - steer
[*][b]Space[/b] - handbrake
[*][b]V[/b] - chase camera on / off
[*][b]Q[/b] - let go
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Signal range 30-500 m: [i]150 m[/i]
[*]Pick-up distance 10-200 m: [i]80 m[/i]
[*]Chase camera on at start: [i]yes[/i]
[*]Camera distance 3-20 m: [i]7 m[/i]
[*]Freeze player while driving: [i]yes[/i]
[*]Mouse sensitivity: [i]x1[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]A car that another player drives cannot be taken
[*]Past the signal range the car brakes and is released after 3 s
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).

[h1]Singularity Grenade[/h1]
A throwable gravity grenade. It bounces, rises and opens into a short-lived singularity that pulls in debris, objects and players, squeezes them into a spinning orbit and then collapses in a blast.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]The pull falls off with distance; heavy objects resist
[*]Objects that reach the core spin on a tight orbit
[*]Tears weak materials around it: glass, wood, plastic, plaster, foliage
[*]Implosion: white flash, outward blast that throws everything it gathered, scorch mark
[*]Up to 4 grenades at once
[*]The Camera Drone mod drops these grenades when both mods are on
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hold LMB, release[/b] - throw (hold up to 1 s to throw farther)
[*][b]RMB[/b] - short toss at your feet
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Radius 3-20 m: [i]8 m[/i]
[*]Strength x0.2-x3: [i]x1[/i]
[*]Duration 1-15 s: [i]4 s[/i]
[*]Grenades 1-50 (unlimited in sandbox): [i]5[/i]
[*]Opens after stopping / on impact: [i]after stopping[/i]
[*]Pulls the player: [i]yes[/i]
[*]Damage to players off / low / high: [i]low[/i]
[*]Tears weak materials: [i]yes[/i]
[*]Real explosion at the end: [i]no[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Metal, concrete, brick and rock are not torn
[*]The number of grenades applies when the level starts
[/list]

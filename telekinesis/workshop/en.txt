[h1]Telekinesis[/h1]
A telekinesis gauntlet. Lift the loose objects around your aim point into a spinning ring in front of you, charge, and throw them as a volley - or put them down gently.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Up to 8 objects at once, each up to 200 kg
[*]They hover on a spinning ring that tightens while you charge
[*]Volley throw at 14-54 m/s depending on the charge
[*]Energy meter (can be switched off)
[*]Violet outlines, wisps, sparkles, a rising hum
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB (hands empty)[/b] - lift the objects around the aim point
[*][b]Hold LMB (holding), release[/b] - charge and throw them as a volley
[*][b]RMB[/b] - put them down gently
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Pick-up radius 1-8 m: [i]3 m[/i]
[*]Max objects 1-20: [i]8[/i]
[*]Heaviest object 10-1000 kg: [i]200 kg[/i]
[*]Throw power: [i]x1[/i]
[*]Reach 5-100 m: [i]40 m[/i]
[*]Energy meter: [i]on[/i]
[*]Energy lasts 5-60 s: [i]15 s[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Vehicles and players are never picked up
[*]Putting the tool away drops everything
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).

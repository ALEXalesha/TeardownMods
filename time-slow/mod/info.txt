en_description = Hold H (or toggle, or pick another key in Options) to slow the world down to x0.5, x0.25 or x0.1 (J switches the speed). The slowdown ramps in and out smoothly, the picture turns blue and less saturated, music is muffled, a heartbeat plays, and whooshes mark entering and leaving slow motion. An energy meter drains while slowed and recharges afterwards (can be turned off). Optional faster walking while slowed. Note: Teardown slows everything, the player included; faster walking is an approximation.

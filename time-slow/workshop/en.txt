[h1]Time Slow[/h1]
Hold a key to slow the whole world down to x0.5, x0.25 or x0.1, with a smooth ramp, a blue tint, muffled music, a heartbeat and an energy meter. Works with any tool in hand.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Smooth ramp in and out
[*]Blue tint and desaturation, restored exactly afterwards
[*]Muffled music, heartbeat, whooshes, a blue vignette
[*]Energy meter that drains and refills (can be turned off)
[*]HUD: hourglass, speed, energy
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]H (hold, or toggle)[/b] - slow motion
[*][b]J[/b] - speed x0.5 / x0.25 / x0.1
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Key: H / Y / T / middle mouse: [i]H[/i]
[*]Mode: hold / toggle: [i]hold[/i]
[*]Starting speed: [i]x0.25[/i]
[*]Ramp time 0.05-1.5 s: [i]0.35 s[/i]
[*]Energy meter: [i]on[/i]
[*]Energy lasts 2-60 s: [i]8 s[/i]
[*]Full recharge 2-60 s: [i]12 s[/i]
[*]Blue tint 0-1: [i]0.8[/i]
[*]Faster walking in slow motion: [i]off[/i]
[*]Hint at level start: [i]show[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]The player is slowed too: the game has no per-object time scale
[*]In multiplayer the time scale is global
[*]World sounds are pitched down by the engine; only the music gets the low-pass
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).

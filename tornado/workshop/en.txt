[h1]Tornado[/h1]
A storm caller. Spawn a tornado at the aim point: it forms in place, then wanders the map or follows your aim, sucking in, spinning, lifting and throwing objects, vehicles and you, and tearing weak buildings.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Up to 2 tornadoes at once
[*]Bending, swaying funnel of smoke, a dust ring, flying debris
[*]Pulls in, spins, lifts and flings; heavy things resist
[*]Vehicles and the player can be lifted; walls shield you
[*]Tears glass, wood and plaster; concrete and metal at higher strength
[*]Wind roar that grows as it comes, thunder, camera shake
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB[/b] - spawn a tornado at the aim point
[*][b]RMB[/b] - dismiss the nearest one
[/list]

[h1]Trampoline Floor[/h1]
Place bounce pads, or make the whole ground bouncy. Players, objects and vehicles bounce back at least as high as they fell, a little higher each time, and jumping on contact sends you even higher.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Up to 8 pads, 3 x 3 m
[*]Global mode: the whole ground around you bounces
[*]Restitution 1.05 by default, capped by a max speed
[*]Jump on contact for extra height
[*]Works for players, loose objects and vehicles
[*]Boing sound, a dust ring, a dipping membrane
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB[/b] - place a pad on flat ground
[*][b]RMB[/b] - remove the nearest pad
[*][b]R[/b] - whole ground bounces on / off
[*][b]Space on contact[/b] - bounce higher
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Bounciness 0.3-1.5: [i]1.05[/i]
[*]Max bounce speed 5-60 m/s: [i]30[/i]
[*]Jump boost on contact 0-15 m/s: [i]+6[/i]
[*]Players / objects / vehicles bounce: [i]yes[/i]
[*]Global mode radius 10-80 m: [i]30 m[/i]
[*]Whole ground bounces at start: [i]no[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Teardown has no per-surface bounciness, so the mod gives the falling speed back upwards
[*]Pads go on flat ground only
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).

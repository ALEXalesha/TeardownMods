[h1]Tsunami[/h1]
A conch horn that calls a tsunami from where you look. A siren warns while the wave rises on the horizon, then a wall of water rolls in, throws cars, carries away objects and you, tears buildings apart, floods the ground and finally runs back.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Warning siren with distance and height of the wave
[*]A 400 m wide, 14 m high wall of water with a foam crest and spray
[*]A current strong enough to throw cars; carries objects, debris and players
[*]Tears wood and brick buildings apart over the whole wave height
[*]Flood behind the wave, then backwash
[*]Any water in the level turns rough during the event and calms down afterwards
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB[/b] - call a tsunami from where you look
[*][b]RMB[/b] - call it off
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Wave height 3-25 m: [i]14 m[/i]
[*]Wave speed 8-35 m/s: [i]18 m/s[/i]
[*]Flood level behind it 0-4 m: [i]1.5 m[/i]
[*]Damage to buildings x0-x3: [i]x1.5[/i]
[*]Warning siren: [i]on[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Mods cannot raise the game's water level, so the wave and the flood are drawn and push like a strong current
[*]The water level is measured from the ground where you stood when you called it: hills and roofs above it stay dry
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).

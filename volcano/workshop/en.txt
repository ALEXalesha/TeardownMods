[h1]Volcano[/h1]
A magma core tool. Raise a volcano on the ground in a few seconds and watch it erupt in phases: rumble and smoke, lava bombs that punch craters and start fires, lava flows, ash, and a final blast that blows the summit off.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]The cone builds up layer by layer, exactly where you clicked
[*]Smoke column, tremors, glowing lava streams and crater
[*]Heavy lava bombs thrown in arcs: real craters through brick, explosions, spreading fires
[*]Lava flows roll down the slopes and burn and break what they touch
[*]Ash falls, the ground shakes, crater light, roar
[*]Final blast takes the summit off and levels everything within 2.5 cone radii; the dead cone stays
[/list]
[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]LMB on the ground[/b] - raise a volcano
[*][b]RMB during the eruption[/b] - final blast now
[*][b]R[/b] - remove the cones
[/list]
[h2]Settings (Mod Manager - the mod - Options)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Size small 22 m / medium 29 m / large 44 m: [i]medium[/i]
[*]Eruption strength x0.5-x2: [i]x1[/i]
[*]Eruption length 20-120 s: [i]45 s[/i]
[*]Fires: [i]on[/i]
[*]Ash fall: [i]on[/i]
[/list]
[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]One volcano erupts at a time; up to 4 dead cones are kept
[*]The cone is placed on the aim point and may cut into uneven ground
[*]Heavy on big maps; lower the strength if the game stutters
[/list]

Own voxel models and synthesized sounds. For Teardown 2.x (#version 2 scripts).
